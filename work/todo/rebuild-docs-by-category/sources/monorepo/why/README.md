# Why Cur8s

This folder contains a four-part essay series that explains the philosophy behind Cur8s:  
why self-hosting matters, what problems every app must solve, why Kubernetes is the most durable way
to solve them, and how Cur8s turns that long-term destination into a usable starting point.

### The Essays

- **[Stop Renting Your Knowledge](0-stop-renting-your-knowldege.md)**  
  Why self-hosting is a high-leverage investment for bootstrappers.

- **[The Eternal Problems of Self-Hosting](1-the-eternal-problems-of-self-hosting.md)**  
  The universal responsibilities every internet-facing system must handle.

- **[Order Over Chaos](2-choose-order-over-chaos.md)**  
  Why Kubernetes—used simply—is the most stable, long-term operating model.

- **[Begin at the End](3-begin-at-the-end.md)**  
  How Cur8s gives you a production-ready foundation shaped by those ideas.

Each essay stands alone, but together they form the reasoning behind the platform choices in Cur8s.

If you want to understand not just **how** Cur8s works, but **why it looks the way it does**, start
here.
